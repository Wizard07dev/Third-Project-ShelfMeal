How it actually works

01

Tell it what you have

Type a list, or upload one photo of your pantry shelf or fridge. Shelfmeal reads what's visible and adds it to your list for you to check.

02

Set a budget, if you have one

Say how much you're willing to spend on anything extra. Zero is a valid answer. Shelfmeal will only suggest additions inside that number.

03

Get one real meal

Not a list of ten options. One meal, with steps, using mostly what you listed, and a plain note on what to buy if anything.
